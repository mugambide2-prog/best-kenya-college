Admission

Join Best Kenya College — start your journey today

Admission Procedures

  1. Choose your preferred course from our Courses page.
  2. Fill out the online enquiry form below or visit the college.
  3. Submit your academic certificates and identification documents.
  4. Pay the registration fee to secure your place.
  5. Attend the orientation session on reporting day.

Entry Requirements

LevelMinimum Requirement
Certificate CoursesKCSE D plain or equivalent
Diploma CoursesKCSE C- (minus) or equivalent
Short CoursesOpen to all — no formal qualification required

Documents Required

  • Copy of KCSE result slip or certificate
  • Copy of National ID or Birth Certificate
  • Two passport-size photographs
  • Filled application form

Intake Periods & Fees

We have three intakes per year: January, May and September.

Fees are payable via M-Pesa Paybill 123456 or bank transfer. Contact the finance office for a detailed fee structure.
